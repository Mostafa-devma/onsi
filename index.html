أُنْس /ʔuns/  ·  noun, Arabic the warmth of being among people you love.

We named our kitchen after a feeling. The table where nobody is in a hurry, the pot that was always big enough for one more. ONSI cooks that feeling every day — slowly, generously, honestly — and sends it to your door while it's still warm.
